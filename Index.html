Message Library

Static templates organized by pain point and format. Every message follows the same frame: observation about their world, implication of what that costs, then a light opening. No product pitch until they ask.

Make it sound like you. These are starting points, not scripts. Read each one out loud before sending. If it sounds like something you would never actually say, change the words until it does. The best message is one that sounds like a real person wrote it in the moment.
